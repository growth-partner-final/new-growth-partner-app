# 06_RULES — locked commercial and security rules

## Qualification and rewards

- Minimum genuine QR collection is ₹1,000 each day for 15 consecutive days.
- Company commission is 10% of QR collection.
- The one-time onboarding reward equals 10% of company commission from the first qualifying 15 days, never 10% of full QR collection.
- ₹15,000 collection produces ₹1,500 company commission and ₹150 onboarding reward. ₹50,000 produces ₹5,000 and ₹500.
- Recurring growth share is distinct: 10% (months 1–6), 5% (months 7–12), and 2% after month 12, subject to programme eligibility.

## Milestones

25 Shops — Official Nexora T-Shirt; 50 Shops — Samsung Tablet; 100 Shops — Branded HP Laptop; 250 Shops — Electric Scooter; 500 Shops — Latest iPhone; 750 Shops — Royal Enfield 350 CC; 1000+ Shops — District Partner SUV Car.

## RLS and integrity

Growth Partners can access only their profile, referrals, referred shops, earnings, and claims. Shop owners can access only their shop. Admin access requires an explicit admin role. Public users cannot read KYC, earnings, payments, or audit information. Referral validation, commission calculations, and service-role actions are server-only. Manual admin changes require audit logs. Live RLS policies are documented here but are not applied in Phase 0.
